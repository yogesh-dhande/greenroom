# Start testing Greenroom

Open `https://greenroom.usespaces.dev/demo` and choose **Organizer**, **Reviewer**, or **Speaker** to sign in to that preconfigured test account; no inbox or password is needed. Use a separate private window or browser profile for each persona so their session cookies do not replace one another. Organizer and Reviewer land at `/admin`, while Speaker lands at `/portal`.

Demo access expires and cannot create accounts or grant roles. If it is unavailable, use `https://greenroom.usespaces.dev/login` with three inboxes or Gmail aliases such as `email+organizer@gmail.com`, `email+reviewer@gmail.com`, and `email+speaker@gmail.com`: a current admin adds the organizer and reviewer from an event's **Team** page, assigns reviewer tracks, and creates the speaker through a CFP submission or **Add speaker**. Each person then opens their emailed magic link in the matching browser context.
